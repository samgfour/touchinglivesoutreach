Our mission

Our mission is to inspire and empower underserved women, men, and children by providing necessities such as food, clothing, and jobs to enable them to recognize their inner talents and gain confidence in their ability to shape a positive future for themselves and their community.

Our vision

We envision an empowered community of financially and economically empowered people who make better decisions, complete their education, get fulfilling jobs, and develop an inner compass to shape their lives in a positive direction.
