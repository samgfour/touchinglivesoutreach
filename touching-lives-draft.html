Our mission

Our mission is to inspire and empower underserved women, men, and children by providing necessities such as food, clothing, and jobs to enable them to recognize their inner strength and potential, while bringing them closer to the Lord through prayer and fellowship.

Our vision

We envision an empowered community of financially and economically empowered people who make better decisions, complete their education, get fulfilling jobs, and develop an increased capacity to serve and lead.
